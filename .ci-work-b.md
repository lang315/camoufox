## Measured (the issue's open question)

fontconfig's `30-metric-aliases.conf` names a substitute and installs nothing. With the bundle's `fonts.conf` in Docker linux/amd64 (Ubuntu 24.04), fonts directory of Liberation only: `fc-match Calibri family` and `fc-match Cambria family` give `Liberation Mono`, the same as the nonexistent `Zzz Not A Font`. With Carlito and Caladea added: `Carlito` and `Caladea`. `Arial` and `Times New Roman` give `Liberation Sans` and `Liberation Serif` in both. (fc-match, not Firefox's own lookup; the issue's real-Firefox probe was not run.) So a machine without Carlito and Caladea does not have the names, and the always-claimed pair was wrong for about 89% of draws.

## Change

One fix in the data all three launchers read, no launcher code:
- `browser/scripts/gen-fonts-json.py`: an alias joins the essential floor only when its target is in every base (and aliases are no longer unioned into the bases it intersects). Of the 17 Linux aliases only `Calibri` (Carlito) and `Cambria` (Caladea) have targets outside the bases.
- `browser/scripts/gen-font-groups.py`: a unit that brings an alias's target brings the alias too (same `ALIASES` table as the floor, imported from `gen-fonts-json.py`), unless the floor already claims it. No manifest edit, so there is no second copy of the table.

Regenerated data, explained: `python/src/camoufox/essential-fonts.json` Linux 274 to 272 names (`Calibri`, `Cambria` out); `font-groups.json` Linux `libreoffice` unit +2 (`Calibri`, `Cambria`).
