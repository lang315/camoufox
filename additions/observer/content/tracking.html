Camoufox Tracking Observer

Not observable (reads invisible to this tool)

The following properties are not tracked by this observer because they are cached by the JavaScript engine or are un-spoofed by design: timezone, locale/Intl (engine-cached); navigator.vendor, navigator.plugins, navigator.deviceMemory, navigator.userAgentData, navigator.mediaDevices.enumerateDevices, navigator.getBattery, navigator.connection (un-spoofed).
